# how I switched to a dumbphone ✧

A cozy little guide to switching from a smartphone to a dumbphone, plus my story of why I did it.

It covers how to switch from an iPhone to a dumbphone or flip phone, the different kinds of dumbphones (minimalist smartphones vs feature phones), my experience with the Unihertz Titan Pocket and the Sunbeam F1, and how I handle texting, group chats and RCS, maps, Uber, music, photos, QR codes and tickets, paying, work apps, and more.
